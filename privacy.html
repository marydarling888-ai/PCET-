Short version: PCET does not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your data. Everything you enter stays only on your device.

What data this app handles

PCET (Patient Cost Estimator Tool) lets you store information you choose to enter, including:

  • Insurance plan details (payer name, deductible, out-of-pocket max, coinsurance, copay)
  • A log of visits or services you choose to record, including provider name, service type, and amounts

Where your data is stored

All data is stored locally on your device only, using your browser/app's local storage. This app has:

  • No backend server
  • No account or sign-in system
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising
  • No third-party data sharing of any kind

The developer of this app has no access to anything you enter. We cannot see, retrieve, back up, or recover your data \u2014 it exists only on your device.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage/data through your device settings, permanently deletes everything you've entered. There is no copy anywhere else to restore from.

Permissions

This app does not require camera, location, contacts, microphone, or any other device permission to function.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Medical disclaimer

PCET provides cost estimates only and is not medical advice. Estimates are based on numbers you enter and standard cost-sharing rules; actual costs may differ. Always verify with your insurance provider.

Changes to this policy

If this app's data practices ever change (for example, if future features described in the app's "What's next" section are built), this page will be updated before those features become active, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that change.
