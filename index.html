Disclaimer

PCET provides estimates only and does not provide medical advice. Estimates are based on the numbers you enter and standard cost-sharing rules; actual claims may differ due to network discounts, bundling, medical necessity rules, and payer processing. Always verify costs with your insurance provider.

All data you enter is stored only on your device and is never sent to any server. The developer has no access to your data.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data will permanently delete your saved plans.

About

PCET (Patient Cost Estimator Tool) — store insurance plans, track deductibles, and compare out-of-pocket costs. Works fully offline.
